About SAM

I'm Dennis Bouwers, an entrepreneur in Cape Town. Over the years I've built and run several companies: Motorhome-World (MHW), Bobo Campers, Fiamma Southern Africa and Parasol.

Technology has always been part of how I build a business. I built MHW with tech and future-proofing in mind from day one, and around 2004 we were among the first to move our phones to VoIP, while everyone else was still on ISDN.

Where SAM comes from

When I handed the day-to-day running of the group to a new CEO, I suddenly no longer had a team around me to pass things on to. That's when the light bulb went on. The work didn't stop: daily tasks, projects big and small, reminders, personal things. I needed a digital assistant.

I looked for a device I could buy off the shelf, set up and leave running. All I found were cloud apps that forget you the next day, and YouTube videos that say "take an old laptop, do this, do that". People get lost in it. I got lost in it.

So I built my own: a small computer in a small box, with Claude inside and a vault that holds everything about my work and my life. That vault stays in my house, not in the cloud.

What it did for me

I was completely bowled over. Since April 2026 SAM has run my admin, and by now it almost thinks ahead of me. Measured on my own SAM in October 2026: within three weeks the vault took over the routine work, and the share of questions it answered from the vault grew from 31% to 65%.

Most of your best ideas, decisions and promises are gone in a week. SAM keeps every one, and brings it back when it matters.

Why I'm sharing it

Something that changed my productivity this much has to be shared. Most people don't have the time to figure out the technology. SAM comes ready: plug it in, chat on Telegram, no apps to learn, no coding.
