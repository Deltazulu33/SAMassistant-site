Try SAM

Pick a business, then ask SAM anything for 15 minutes, as if it were yours. Who owes you money, what's on this week, draft that quote. It all happens on Telegram, just like a real SAM.

You need Telegram on your phone or computer. Not yet? Get it free from telegram.org/apps, sign up with your cell number, then come back here.

The button opens Telegram straight in the SAM demo, with your code filled in.

How it works

  1. Type your code, the whole code like SAM-1234, and press Start the demo.
  2. Telegram opens the SAM demo. Press Start at the bottom.
  3. Pick a business (personal trainer, plumber, estate agent and more) by its number.
  4. Ask anything for 15 minutes. All the data is made up, so try whatever you like.
