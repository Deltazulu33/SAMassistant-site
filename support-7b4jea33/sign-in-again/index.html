Sign in to Claude again

From anywhere, on your phone, with /login. About 2 minutes. Needs SAM version 0.5 or later.

When you need it

  • SAM warns you 10, 5 and 2 days before its Claude login runs out.
  • SAM tells you it is logged out of Claude and can't answer.
  • SAM only answers "Acknowledged" and nothing else for a long time (it can also be your usage limit: that resets by itself within a few hours).

What you need

  • Your phone with Telegram, the chat with your SAM.
  • The email and password of the Claude account your SAM uses (the one you chose at setup, step 6).

You do not need to be at home: this works on mobile data, anywhere.

Steps

  1. In the chat with your SAM, send /login.
  2. SAM replies "Signing in: the link follows in a few seconds". It then hands over to its sign-in helper, which sends Getting a sign-in link from Claude... and then the link.
  3. Open the link. Sign in with the Claude account your SAM uses and press Authorize.
  4. Claude shows a code. Copy all of it and send it to SAM as a normal message.
  5. The code disappears from the chat once it is used. You get Signed in. The Claude login is valid until date.
  6. SAM starts again and answers within about a minute.

When does my login run out?

Ask SAM: "When does my Claude login expire?" It answers with the date and time. Claude sets this date with every sign-in (about 4 weeks), so a new sign-in can end a little earlier than the old one. That is normal.

If it does not work

  • No link arrives within a minute: send /login again.
  • "Sign-in did not work: Claude did not accept the code": the code must be sent whole and within a few minutes. Send /login for a new link and try again.
  • You took longer than 15 minutes: the link expires. If SAM was still working, it carries on with the old login. Send /login again when you are ready.
  • Wrong Claude account: sign in with the account SAM was set up with. Another account works too, but then SAM uses that subscription.
  • At home you can also use the setup page: ask SAM for "the setup page link", open it (password: the clearance code on the label), step 6, Sign in again.

Only the owner's own Telegram chat can sign SAM in. Others who use your SAM are told it is busy and back shortly.
